# Open research questions — ordered by potential player impact

Last triaged 2026-10-23. Priority refers to **usefulness for progression decisions**, not intrinsic curiosity. A question is not answered merely because someone has repeated an earlier AI assertion.

| ID | Priority | Question | What would resolve it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `TB-OPEN-001` | High | Which bonus categories truly stack additively, and which act as separate damage/proc stages? | Controlled item/artifact swaps, battle logs, official source if available |
| `TB-OPEN-002` | High | When does including an additional lower-tier squad increase actual XP per marginal resource cost? | Replicated with/without tier trials on matched Epic state |
| `TB-OPEN-003` | High | How much value does an added captain contribute through Leadership, equipment, artifacts, and bonus effects relative to their share of distributed XP? | Controlled 2/3/4-leader tests with costs and hero presence recorded |
| `TB-OPEN-004` | Medium | Does HP removed explain total XP uniformly across different Epic events, and how is event scaling applied? | Paired HP/XP logs over events |
| `TB-OPEN-005` | Medium | Which official policy language covers direct server-command tools, shield-drop detection, or automated client controls? | Original dated in-game notices |
| `TB-OPEN-006` | Low for Epic XP | Does Coliseum's Attack Value correlate to PvP damage, and can it reveal portable bonus terms across modes? | Controlled Coliseum loadout experiment; no PvE transfer assumed |
| `TB-OPEN-007` | Conditional | Could future PvP rewards materially change late-game captain XP allocation? | Dated reward/XP changes, not speculation alone |

To propose an experiment, open an issue using [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) and link the claim IDs that a result would test.
