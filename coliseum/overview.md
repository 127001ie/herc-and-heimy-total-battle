# Coliseum: reported configuration and strategic questions

**Claims:** `TB-COL-001`, `TB-COL-002`, `TB-COL-003`, `TB-COL-004`  
**Reviewed:** 2026-10-23  
**Source maturity:** firsthand player description; official in-game screenshots not yet attached.

## Arena and matching (`TB-COL-001`; observed_player)

Reported matchmaking is grouped by **unlocked troop level**, e.g. G9/S9, rather than directly by Might. Five captains are selected with equipment, artifacts, and troop selection; combat resembles standard PvP combat. The interface includes quick selection of captains/equipment/artifacts and preset top-tier Guardsmen, Specialists, and Monsters.

## Attack Value (`TB-COL-002`; observed_player)

The Coliseum shows a newly introduced **single Attack Value number for the entire selected five-captain army**. It is not reported as a separate statistic for each captain or troop type. How this metric is computed, and whether it predicts real PvP damage or anything about PvE, is **unknown**.

## Reward structure (`TB-COL-003`; observed_player; partial uncertainty)

Reported rewards include a city skin giving +20% Strength, Artemisia fragments, a frame believed to give approximately +5% (not verified), a PvP ranking title with uncertain eligibility threshold, Conquest Points, and other items. Eligibility, duration, and exact scopes should be checked in the current in-game interface.

## Artemisia (`TB-COL-004`; observed_player)

Player reports describe Artemisia's relevant bonus as similar to Beowulf's but **restricted to PvP**. She is reported to share the Hall of Fame Courage group with Hercules and Heimdall. Exact numeric abilities, all role nuances, and future content relevance need independent documentation.

## Decision relevance

Players may acquire time-gated fragments without immediately investing XP. This can preserve future options if PvP incentives change, but it has opportunity costs against immediate permanent strength rewards. Which is best depends on account stage and personal goals. PvP Attack Value must not be naively extrapolated to PvE mechanics.
