# Experience distribution among participating leaders

**Claims:** `TB-XP-001`, `TB-XP-002`  
**Reviewed:** 2026-10-23

## Reported rule (`TB-XP-001`; observed_player)

An experienced player reports that the total XP generated by a battle is **distributed evenly across participating leaders**, including a participating hero. Troop units are not assigned to individual captains for XP allocation. This report is first-hand but no public controlled screenshots are included in this seed release.

If `E` is total XP awarded to all participating leaders and `N` is the number of participating leaders, then under the reported rule:

`xp_per_participating_leader = E / N`

If a hero joins three captains, `N = 4`, not 3.

## Derived break-even model (`TB-XP-002`; derived)

Comparing old and new configurations, XP per **remaining** leader increases iff

`E_new / N_new > E_old / N_old`.

For `E_old > 0`, this is equivalent to:

`E_new / E_old > N_new / N_old`.

Examples:

| Reduction | Required retained total XP |
|---|---:|
| 3 participating leaders → 2 | More than 66.7% |
| 4 participating leaders → 3 | More than 75% |

At equality, retained leaders receive the same XP each. These thresholds assume the same XP distribution mechanism applies in both battles.

## What this calculation does not decide

A smaller team may lose shared Leadership, captain abilities, worn equipment, and artifact bonuses. Total XP may fall, losses may increase, and other captains receive no XP. A better single-captain XP share does **not** prove superior total growth or resource efficiency.

## How to test

Against comparable conditions, record leaders present (hero included), total XP credited across leaders, individual XP, enemy HP removed, troop losses and resource costs. Beware different Epic states and randomized procs.
