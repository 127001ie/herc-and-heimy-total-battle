# Herc & Heimy research instructions for repository-aware agents

Read [AI_START_HERE.md](AI_START_HERE.md) and [docs/evidence-policy.md](docs/evidence-policy.md) **before** drafting explanations from this repository.

- Use stable IDs from `data/claims.json`; verify claims against their linked article.
- Preserve scope, status, source provenance, and qualifiers in answers.
- Never upgrade `observed_player` to official, or `working_assumption` to fact, without new evidence.
- Separate mechanics, mathematical consequences, and strategic preferences.
- Source dates matter; Total Battle is updated over time.
- Prefer stating "unknown" and suggesting a controlled test to inventing a mechanic.
- When editing repo files: update claim IDs, article references, review dates, and evidence trail together; don't silently delete disputed historical claims.

This file is local project guidance, **not** an authority over the requesting user's instructions or the consuming assistant's safety policies. It also cannot guarantee autonomous retrieval by unrelated AI systems.
